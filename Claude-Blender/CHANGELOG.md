# Changelog — Lone Hand, Blender trial

All notable changes to this trial are listed here, newest first.
The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/); the numbers follow
[Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/). The trial is numbered apart from Lone Hand (see `VERSIONING.md`).

## [0.3.0] - 2026-11-28

The landmarks of the three chapters, redrawn.

### Added

- **Dry Gulch: the train is new.** The engine has a banded boiler, a flared stack, domes, a bell, a headlamp that
  glows, spoked wheels and a cow-catcher of slats; behind it a tender heaped with coal, a boarded boxcar and a
  coach with lit windows.
- **Dry Gulch: the Silver Queen mine has a headframe** with its wheel, on top of the outcrop, and an ore cart
  stands on the rails outside.
- **Dry Gulch: the church, the station and the water tower.** Corner boards, a frieze, door and window frames with
  pointed hoods on the church; a water tank of staves bound with iron hoops.
- **Kagemori: the shrine, the pagoda and the castle.** Rafters under every curved roof, plaster walls between dark
  timbers.
- **Uri: the chapel, the keep and the mill.** Stone plinths, quoins up the corners, lintels and sills round doors
  and windows.

### Changed

- **The foot of the title screen is tidied up.** Sound and graphics on the left, the game for two in the middle,
  the version on the right, in one row that no longer runs into the chapters or over itself, whatever the size of
  the window. "Choose a chapter" now stands above the chapters.
- Around the headframe and the ore cart you now walk round four posts, a winding drum and the cart.
- The game file is 8.8 MB (0.2.0: 7.9 MB).

### Removed

- **Dry Gulch: the mission is gone.** The white mission south-west of town was a second church beside the first; the
  chapter here has the wooden church only, and the train now comes in across open desert. Lone Hand keeps its mission.

## [0.2.0] - 2026-11-28

Everything that stands in the three chapters now comes from Blender, and the trial shows only what it changed.

### Added

- **The other buildings, reworked in Blender.** The church, the mission, the station, the barn, the water tower
  and the mine of Dry Gulch; the shrine, the pagoda, the castle, the gates and the bridges of Kagemori; the
  chapel, the keep, the mill, the camp and the landing of Uri; and with them the fences, the stairs and the
  furniture: 3 650 shapes in all. Wooden walls, floors and doors are made of boards you can tell apart, edges are
  planed off, roofs have a thickness, and shade and dirt gather where things meet.
- These reworked walls can still be shot through and broken, as in Lone Hand.

### Changed

- **Only Dry Gulch, Kagemori and Uri can be played.** The six chapters that have nothing from Blender are greyed
  out on the title screen and are no longer offered for a game for two; play them in Lone Hand.
- The game file is 7.9 MB (0.1.0: 5.4 MB).

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-28

The first trial: Lone Hand 2.7.0 with the people and the houses of three chapters modelled in Blender.

### Added

- **Dry Gulch, Kagemori and Uri: people from Blender.** Every figure of the three chapters (enemies, heroes,
  duel villains, bystanders: 39 looks) is dressed from one library of Blender parts: bodies in one piece instead
  of separate blocks, knees that bend, hats with curled brims, coats and skirts that follow the legs, armour,
  helmets, beards and hairstyles. They move, aim, fall and are dressed in random colours as before.
- **Dry Gulch: Main Street from Blender.** The twelve houses of the street, inside and out: clapboard walls,
  cornices on brackets, window frames, porches on rafters, the balconies of the saloon and the hotel, the flat
  roofs with their ladders.
- **Kagemori: the six houses from Blender.** Timber frames, verandas, mat floors, tiled and thatched roofs with
  their curved eaves and ridges. The paper screens are still the game's, and can still be broken.
- **Uri: the chalets and the barns from Blender.** Squared logs crossing at the corners, stone ground floors,
  balconies, shutters, roofs on purlins, firewood stacked by the wall.
- `?classic` in the address shows the game without the Blender models; `?level=1&pair` (or 2, 3) puts every
  look of the chapter in front of you, old and new side by side.

### Changed

- The walls of the Blender houses cannot be shot through or broken.
- The game file is 5.4 MB (Lone Hand 2.7.0: 2.2 MB).
